# Changelog

## 1.0.0 (2026-10-08)

First version, for Minecraft 1.20.1, Forge 47 and Ad Astra 1.15.21 or newer.

- With an Ad Astra space suit, netherite space suit or jet suit on, first-person arms are drawn as normal arms instead of Ad Astra's misplaced suit arms. TaCZ guns, the empty hand and Ad Astra's zip gun all use the normal arms.
- The suit still shows in third person and to other players.
- Logs one line at startup that says whether the fix applied.
- Client-only. A server with the mod starts normally, and clients and servers don't need to match.
