# Changelog

## 1.0.0 for Minecraft 1.21.1 (2026-11-03)

First version for Minecraft 1.21.1, on NeoForge 21.1 and Fabric, for Ad Astra 1.16.26 or newer. The same fix as the 1.20.1 version.

- Tested with [UNOFFICIAL] TaCZ NeoForge Port 1.1.8-hotfix-r7 and TaCZ: Refabricated 0.7.1.
- Client-only. A server with the mod starts normally, and clients and servers don't need to match.

## 1.0.0 (2026-10-08)

First version, for Minecraft 1.20.1, Forge 47 and Ad Astra 1.15.21 or newer.

- With an Ad Astra space suit, netherite space suit or jet suit chestplate on, first-person arms are drawn as normal arms. TaCZ's third-person gun animations no longer land on them, so guns are held properly while aiming, reloading and running.
- The empty hand and Ad Astra's zip gun use the normal arms too. The suit still shows in third person and to other players.
- Logs one line at startup that says whether the fix applied.
- Client-only. A server with the mod starts normally, and clients and servers don't need to match.
